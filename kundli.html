Methodology & Accuracy

This kundli tool combines simple astronomical approximations and classical numerology to produce an accessible, educational birth report. Sun sign (Zodiac) is determined from the calendar day and month using standard tropical boundaries. The Moon sign and Nakshatra (birth star) are approximated by estimating the Moon's mean motion from an epoch date to the provided birth date; this provides a useful, general Rashi/Nakshatra for learning and quick readings but is not a substitute for a full ephemeris-based chart.

Mulank (birth number) and name-number calculations use traditional numerology rules: Mulank is derived from the day of birth (with double-digit reduction rules applied as shown on the page), and name numbers map letters to digits using a simple Pythagorean mapping. These methods highlight personality tendencies and suggest practical areas of focus, but they deliberately avoid claiming deterministic fate.

Important accuracy notes: precise astrological charts require the exact geographic coordinates (longitude & latitude) and accurate time-zone/ daylight-saving offsets to compute house placements and planet positions to arc-second accuracy. This lightweight generator does not perform full planetary ephemeris calculations or house system placements — features commonly found in professional astrology software. As such, expect the results to be indicative and educational rather than precise for technical chart work.

How to use the results: treat this report as a clear starting point for self-reflection and learning. The Mulank, Name Number, Sun and Moon sign descriptions describe tendencies and influences, not certainties. If you need an exact birth-chart reading (for medical, legal, financial, or major life decisions), consult a professional astrologer who can compute a full natal chart from precise coordinates and an authoritative ephemeris.

Privacy & data: no data leaves your browser — all calculations run locally and temporary results are stored only if you use the site's local-history features. We provide this tool for guidance and entertainment and do not offer professional or certified advice.
