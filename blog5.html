How Your Name Affects Your Destiny – Numerology Guide

Have you ever wondered why some names feel powerful, lucky, or attractive? Why do some people achieve success effortlessly while others struggle, even with the same level of hard work? According to numerology, your name is not just a label — it is a powerful energy signature that influences your destiny, personality, opportunities, and vibration.

Tip: Use your full birth name to calculate your Name Number for the most accurate reading. Nicknames and pen names may shift certain vibrations.

What Is Name Numerology?

Name numerology is the science of converting letters of your name into numbers to understand your natural strengths, weaknesses, life purpose, and destiny. Every alphabet vibrates at a certain frequency, and when they combine to form your name, they create your unique energy pattern.

The Numerology Chart (Pythagorean System)

Each letter corresponds to a number:

  • A J S = 1
  • B K T = 2
  • C L U = 3
  • D M V = 4
  • E N W = 5
  • F O X = 6
  • G P Y = 7
  • H Q Z = 8
  • I R = 9

How to Calculate Your Name Number

  1. Write your full name.
  2. Convert each letter to its number using the chart.
  3. Add all numbers.
  4. Reduce to a single digit unless it becomes a Master Number (11, 22, or 33).

Example: Name – R A H U L

R = 9   A = 1   H = 8   U = 3   L = 3
Total = 24 → 2 + 4 = 6
✔ Name Number of Rahul = 6
